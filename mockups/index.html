VibeDrive.je — Controls Mockup Tournament

3 design philosophies · pick your winner or hybrid

How to review

  • Can I reach all controls with my thumbs on iPad?
  • Is the 3D view maximized (minimal HUD clutter)?
  • Can I switch vehicle mode in 1 tap?
  • Can I change camera in 1 tap or gesture?
  • Are secondary features (bus, radio, leaderboard) accessible but out of the way?
  • Does it look good and on-brand?

Tip: each mockup has iPad + Mobile toggle buttons inside it.
