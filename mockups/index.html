How to Review

Each mockup below shows a different control layout philosophy. Tap inside each frame to interact. When you find your favorite (or want a hybrid), tell Vibe which elements you want.

  • Can I reach all controls with my thumbs on iPad?
  • Is the 3D view maximized (minimal HUD clutter)?
  • Can I switch vehicle mode in 1 tap?
  • Can I change camera in 1 tap or gesture?
  • Are secondary features (bus, radio, leaderboard) accessible but not in the way?
  • Does it look good? Is it on-brand?
