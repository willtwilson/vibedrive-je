# ⚡ Vibe Drive Jersey — Brand Identity

## Brand Essence
**Drive Jersey. Feel Jersey.**  
A 3D driving game that puts you on real Jersey terrain — live weather, real tides, actual daylight. Not a generic map game. A love letter to the island, built in 90 minutes.

## Brand Personality
- **Bold** — not subtle. Jersey doesn't apologise. Neither do we.
- **Fast** — built in a sprint, plays at speed, presents with energy
- **Local** — every data point is Jersey-specific. This is OUR island.
- **Playful but engineered** — it's fun, but the tech is real

## Visual System

### Colors
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Jersey Green** | `#00A86B` | Primary brand — the island's fields, racing green heritage |
| **Atlantic Blue** | `#0A1A3F` | Deep background — the sea around Jersey at night |
| **Sprint Yellow** | `#FFD60A` | Accent / energy — hackathon speed, headlights, speed lines |
| **Surf White** | `#F0F4FF` | Text on dark — sea foam, cloud, clean contrast |
| **Cliff Grey** | `#2A2A3E` | Surface / cards — Jersey granite, secondary surfaces |
| **Rally Red** | `#E63946` | The car, danger, brake lights — pure adrenaline |

### Typography
- **Display / Headlines:** `Inter` weight 800-900, uppercase, tight tracking (-0.02em) — bold, industrial, automotive
- **Body / UI:** `Inter` weight 400-500, normal tracking — clean, readable
- **Mono / Data:** `JetBrains Mono` weight 500 — speedometer, coordinates, technical labels

### Shape Language
- **Angular.** Zero border-radius on primary surfaces. Sharp corners = precision = engineering.
- **Pill buttons** for interactive controls (32px radius) — touch-friendly, game-like
- **No soft shadows.** Depth through color contrast and layering, not elevation.

### Motion Posture
- Fast snaps, not slow eases. 0.2s transitions. This is a driving game — everything moves.
- Slide transitions: slide horizontally (driving metaphor), 0.3s
- No decorative animation. Motion serves navigation or state change.

### Logo Treatment
- **⚡** lightning bolt as primary mark — speed, energy, the vibe.je hackathon
- Wordmark: `VIBE DRIVE` in Inter 900 uppercase, `JERSEY` in Inter 400 uppercase with wide tracking
- Tagline: `Real island. Real data. Real fun.`

### Layout Rules
- Full-bleed dark backgrounds (Atlantic Blue `#0A1A3F`)
- Jersey Green as accent lines, highlights, active states
- Sprint Yellow for key numbers, stats, CTAs
- Generous whitespace — let the bold type breathe
- No cards unless content genuinely needs containment
- Screenshots and imagery are full-bleed, edge-to-edge

## Tone of Voice
- Short sentences. Punchy. No filler.
- "Drive Jersey." not "Experience the beauty of driving around Jersey."
- Numbers over adjectives: "12-metre tides" not "massive tides"
- Jersey pride without parochialism — it's world-class, it just happens to be here

## Do
- Use Jersey Green as the hero colour
- Keep backgrounds dark (Atlantic Blue)
- Let type do the heavy lifting — big, bold, uppercase
- Use Sprint Yellow sparingly for impact
- Keep it fast and energetic

## Don't
- Don't use gradients (except subtle dark-to-darker)
- Don't use rounded cards or soft shadows
- Don't use emoji decoratively in the brand system (⚡ is the exception)
- Don't dilute with pastel colours
- Don't use more than 3 colours on any single surface
